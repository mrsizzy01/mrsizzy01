### 1. DRC Market

An online marketplace project focused on making digital commerce more accessible in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

The platform aims to connect buyers and sellers through an accessible online shopping experience.

**Focus:** E-commerce, product listings, digital commerce, APIs, and marketplace development.

### 2. ISAM Lubumbashi

A university management platform concept designed to support academic and administrative processes, including student records, courses, grades, payments, and user roles.

**Focus:** Education technology, academic management, database design, and business software.

### 3. KONGO

A digital infrastructure concept exploring identity, professional presence, trust, and economic activity in a connected digital environment.

**Focus:** Digital identity, business networks, and African technology.

### 4. SOFIMMO

A vehicle rental management project designed to support the digitalization of vehicle rental services and business operations.

**Focus:** Vehicle rental, business management, and digital services.

### 5. Wedding Invitation Platform

A digital invitation platform concept designed to help couples create personalized wedding invitations, manage guest confirmations, share event information, and organize guest experiences.

**Focus:** Web applications, event management, QR codes, and guest management.
